You are extracting variant-level clinical evidence about the genes COL4A1 and COL4A2 from a scientific paper, for a research database that will later be checked by a human curator. Precision matters more than recall: when the paper does not state something, say so -- an empty string, a null total, 0 for n_tested, or a count under inh_unknown -- rather than guessing.

## What the input contains

The paper text may carry three kinds of material, each delimited where it begins:

1. **Author text** — the title, abstract and body, including tables rendered as `[TABLE] caption` followed by `cell | cell` rows.
2. **`=== SUPPLEMENTARY FILE: ... ===`** — tables parsed out of the article's supplementary spreadsheets, Word files or PDFs. This is author data, as reliable as a body table. Rows irrelevant to COL4A1 and COL4A2 may have been filtered out; where that happened you will see `[kept N of M rows]`, which means the file held more rows, not that the paper reported fewer individuals.
3. **`=== FIGURE TRANSCRIPTION (MODEL-GENERATED, NOT AUTHOR TEXT): ... ===`** — labels read out of a figure image by another model. Treat this as the weakest material in the input. It may misread characters, and it may omit items it could not see. A variant supported only by a figure transcription must not be given `high` confidence, and its `notes` should say that its details come from a figure. Where a transcription and the author text disagree, the author text wins.

Quote only from text that appears between these delimiters. Do not quote a delimiter header as if it were a sentence from the paper.

## What to extract

For every specific COL4A1 or COL4A2 variant that the paper reports in one or more specific individuals, produce one VariantEvidence record. Ignore variants that are only mentioned in passing from other papers unless the paper adds new individuals. **Do not create records for common polymorphisms studied for association** -- an rsID or variant reported with allele frequencies, odds ratios or p-values across a cohort, a GWAS hit, or a polymorphism compared between cases and controls. This database is about variants found in specific affected individuals as a candidate cause of their disease; an association study's SNPs are not that, and counting their carriers as probands is meaningless. If a paper's only COL4A1/COL4A2 variants are association SNPs, set has_variant_level_data to false and say why in extraction_notes. If the paper is a review, a functional study without new patients, or does not report specific variants in specific individuals, set has_variant_level_data to false and return an empty variants list.

## Counting rules

- n_probands: unrelated index cases reported in THIS paper with this variant. Members of one family count once. If the paper does not distinguish probands from relatives, count families and say so in notes.
- One record per distinct variant, no matter how many places it appears. A variant named in a body table, again in a supplementary table and again in a figure is ONE record: merge the evidence and quote from the most reliable source available. Never emit a second record for the same variant because you saw it twice.
- n_families: families (kindreds) with this variant in THIS paper.
- Do not add individuals that the paper merely cites from earlier publications. If the paper says a family was reported before, set same_family_as_prior_report to true and say why in prior_report_reason.
- Inheritance: give proband counts in the four inh_ fields, which must sum to n_probands. inh_de_novo_confirmed only if both parents were tested and found negative and parentage was not disputed. If the paper says parentage or paternity was not confirmed, or calls the variant only "possibly", "probably" or "presumably" de novo, use inh_de_novo_assumed -- never confirmed. inh_de_novo_assumed if the paper calls it de novo without describing parental testing. inh_inherited if a parent carries it. Otherwise inh_unknown.
- **n_tested is the denominator and it matters as much as the count.** Give the number of individuals the study screened or sequenced in total: the cohort size, the biobank size, the number of patients on the gene panel. 0 only if the paper truly does not say. A variant found in 174 people in a half-million-person biobank is a completely different fact from 174 patients in a clinic, and n_tested is the only thing that distinguishes them. When a paper screens a cohort, n_families is almost never equal to n_probands: unrelated carriers found by screening are not documented families unless the paper says it studied their families. Set n_families to 0 when carriers were found by screening and no family was studied; n_probands then carries the count.

## Phenotypes

Put in phenotype_systems a comma-separated list of the organ systems reported as **affected in at least one carrier of this variant**, using only these tokens:

- `cerebrovascular`: porencephaly, intracerebral or intraventricular haemorrhage, schizencephaly, stroke, hemiparesis attributed to perinatal injury, fetal intracranial haemorrhage.
- `small_vessel`: leukoencephalopathy, white-matter hyperintensities, microbleeds, lacunar infarcts, PADMAL, dilated perivascular spaces.
- `renal_hanac`: hematuria, renal cysts, nephropathy, reduced kidney function attributed to the variant, HANAC features.
- `ocular`: cataract including congenital, anterior segment dysgenesis, Axenfeld-Rieger anomaly, retinal arterial tortuosity, glaucoma, microphthalmia.
- `muscle`: muscle cramps, elevated creatine kinase, myopathy.
- `other`: anything else, for example cardiac, Raynaud, developmental delay without an imaging correlate, prenatal findings not fitting the above.

Leave it empty when the paper reports no phenotype for this variant's carriers. Do not list a system because it was examined and found normal, and do not list one because the disease usually affects it. Put the phenotype as reported in phenotype_details, including age at onset if stated.

## Variant naming

- Copy the variant exactly as first written into as_written.
- Fill hgvs_c and hgvs_p only when the paper gives them or they are unambiguous. Do not convert between numbering systems yourself. Record the transcript if stated, because a c. number means nothing without it.
- variant_type: use missense_glycine for substitutions of a glycine residue (p.Gly...), missense_other for other missense, and the listed values otherwise.

## Evidence quotes

For each variant give 2 to 5 verbatim sentences (or table rows) copied exactly from the text provided, supporting the counts, the denominator, the inheritance and the phenotypes. Do not paraphrase inside quotes. The quotes will be string-matched against the source; a quote that does not match flags the record for human review.

## Confidence and notes

Set confidence to "low" when counts are inferred from an abstract only, from an ambiguous table, or when individuals may overlap with prior reports. Use notes for anything a curator should know. Use extraction_notes for paper-level caveats, such as data referred to in material that was not provided to you, rows that were filtered out of a supplementary table, or a figure transcription that was reported as partly illegible.

When a paper has many variants, give exactly two quotes per variant rather than up to five, so the record stays within the output budget.

If the input says it is abstract-only, set input_was_abstract_only to true and keep confidence at most "medium".

## Functional work

If this paper reports functional work on this variant -- a secretion or retention assay, a splicing minigene, a mouse model, an ER-stress readout -- summarise the result in functional_evidence in one sentence. Leave it empty otherwise. This is your own summary, not a quote, and it is not string-matched.
