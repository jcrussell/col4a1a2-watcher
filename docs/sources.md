# Data sources, rates and licences

Every host below is reached through `src/col4a1a2/http.py`. Rates are configured in `config/col4a1a2.toml`; anything not listed defaults to one request per second.

## In use

| Source | What we take | Rate | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Europe PMC REST | Search results, abstracts, author lists and affiliations, open-access full text as JATS XML, reference lists, metadata by PMID (`EXT_ID`, never `PMID` -- there is no such field) | 2/s | Metadata is open. Article licences vary per paper and are stated in the XML. |
| Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles` | One ZIP per paper holding supplementary files and figure image renditions | 0.25/s | Same article licences. The ZIP is built per request, so it gets its own much slower bucket; at 2/s it returns errors. Members stay in the untracked bundle store and are never redistributed. |
| PubTator 3 (NCBI) | Entity-aware search, BioC annotations, variant mentions linked to a gene | 2/s | Public NCBI resource. |
| NCBI E-utilities | Full-text fallback, ClinVar record counts | 2/s | Public. Requests carry a contact address, as NCBI asks. |
| Anthropic API | Triage on title and abstract, figure transcription, extraction on full text, adversarial review | n/a | Outputs belong to the account holder. |

## Deliberately not used

- **AlphaGenome and the AlphaGenome Atlas.** The terms forbid using outputs to train other machine-learning models and bar clinical decision-making. The regulatory site most relevant to one disease mechanism in these genes is not modelled. Nothing in scope needs it.
- **AlphaMissense.** Licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which would constrain redistribution of anything derived from it, and the current scope makes no use of a missense pathogenicity score.
- **LOVD.** Its terms prohibit scripted download and grant no licence to store or redistribute its records; see `AGENTS.md`. Nothing from it is fetched, cached or derived.
- Hosts unreachable from the development sandbox and not needed: Zenodo, HuggingFace, DECIPHER, MGI, UCSC downloads, AlphaFold, RCSB, GTEx.

## Parsing dependencies

All permissively licensed and compatible with the project's BSD-3-Clause: `openpyxl` (MIT) for spreadsheets, `python-docx` (MIT) for Word, `pdfplumber` (MIT) for PDFs. PyMuPDF was rejected: it is AGPL-3.0 and would conflict. `pdfplumber` brings Pillow and pypdfium2, which means a scanned PDF could later be rendered to an image and read with vision without adding anything new.

## Licensing of our own output

- **Full texts and supplementary files are stored locally and never redistributed.** This is why `data/raw/`, `data/cache/` and `data/responses/` (raw model output, whose thinking can paraphrase article text) are untracked, and why test fixtures are limited to papers under a licence that permits redistribution, with attribution recorded in `tests/fixtures/README.md`. Supplement bytes carry the article's licence, vary per paper, and can hold third-party or identifiable material, so they never become a test fixture; the supplement fixtures are synthetic, generated by `col4a1a2-dev fixtures`.
- **Extracted records** carry a PMID, a prepared citation and short verbatim quotes. Attribution per row is both a licence obligation and the thing that makes the table checkable.
- **The evidence and literature tables** are released CC BY 4.0. Annotations obtained from other services are not redistributed with it; only identifiers and coordinates.
- **Code** is BSD-3-Clause (`LICENSE`).
- Research use only. Machine-extracted, spot-checked at a stated rate, not clinical advice.
